# Blinded Human Scoring Packet

Strategy-masked single-rater scoring; familiarity and repeated images limit masking.

Mark each defined fact Yes / No / Unsure and quote supporting words. Equivalent wording counts. Wrong subjects or units do not satisfy the fact. Empty text conveys zero facts.

List distinct unsupported assertions with evidence; an omitted fact is not an invented claim. Correct unit conversions are allowed. Assess all four defects separately; a missing reference fact does not automatically make the whole description uninformative.

Word limits are assessed independently from unnecessary verbosity. Resolve all Unsure entries before unblinding. Do not open the private mapping until review is finalized.

Scoring clarification approved November 6, 2026, after held-out collection: relationship facts require the relationship to be expressed; listing values alone does not satisfy them. Equivalent relational wording counts, regardless of fact number. Unit abbreviations such as ms count as stated units.

Defect 3.3 checks whether essential information is missing or cannot be established. A missing summary sentence alone is not a defect: a straightforward comparison of a short, complete, clearly ordered value sequence may supply sufficient information. A relationship fact No and 3.3 No are allowed. Do not choose marks merely to avoid overlap. Record uncertainty for complex or ambiguous presentations.
