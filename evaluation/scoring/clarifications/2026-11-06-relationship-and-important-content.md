## Agreed interpretation

- A relationship fact requires the relationship to be expressed. Equivalent
  relational wording counts; a particular keyword is unnecessary. A value list
  alone does not convey a relationship fact under this operational definition.
  This applies regardless of the fact's number; F3 is not always a relationship.
- Defect 3.3 asks whether essential information is missing or cannot be
  established. A missing summary sentence alone is not a defect. A short,
  complete, clearly ordered sequence may communicate sufficient information
  through a straightforward comparison. Lists are not automatically sufficient:
  incomplete values, a range without ordering, or complex presentations may not
  establish the essential information. Record evidence and use Unsure when needed.
- Fact coverage and 3.3 are separate judgments. Do not force agreement or choose
  a score to avoid double counting. A relationship fact No and 3.3 No are allowed.
- Unit abbreviations such as ms count as stated units. Missing coverage is not
  an unsupported claim. Do not require an opinion or causal explanation.
